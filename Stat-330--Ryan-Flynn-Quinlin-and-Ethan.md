We are interested in the contributing factors in flight delays. We want to be able to predict how delayed a flight will be upon arrival given the intended distance of the flight, the air time, the day of the week it leaves, its departure delay, the airline, how many flights flew that day, and whether or not it’s coming from a major US city or hub. This is intended to be useful for anyone who flies, in order to tell their relatives when to plan on picking them up. We care because this model could inform airline and city pairing choices when booking flights, in order to minimize delays.
